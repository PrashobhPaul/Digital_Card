Delete your RVonWheelz account and data

RVonWheelz is a non-profit car & bike pooling app for the residents of RVE, Nacharam. This page explains how to request deletion of your RVonWheelz account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to prashobhpaulnambadan@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to RVonWheelz.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my RVonWheelz account.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account and associated personal data.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation once your account has been deleted.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

DataWhat happens
Name, email address, phone numberDeleted
Profile photo (avatar) and office locationDeleted
Rides you offered or bookedDeleted
In-app chat messagesDeleted
Notification/device identifiersDeleted

What may be kept

We may retain a limited amount of information where we are legally required to, or to prevent fraud and abuse. Any such data is kept only for as long as necessary and is then deleted. We do not sell your personal data, and RVonWheelz contains no advertising.
