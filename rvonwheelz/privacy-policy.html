RVonWheelz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

RVonWheelz ("the app", "we", "us") is a non-profit car and bike pooling app for residents of the RVE, Nacharam society. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It is operated by Prashobh Paul on behalf of the community. For any privacy question or request, contact prashobhpaulnambadan@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to let neighbours find and coordinate shared rides:

  • Account information — your name/display name, email address, and (if you provide it) phone number, used to create your account and let riders identify and contact each other.
  • Profile information — an optional avatar image and your usual office location, used to match and display rides.
  • Ride information — rides you offer or book, including pickup and drop points (entered as text/landmarks), timing, and seat requests.
  • Messages — in-app chat messages you send to coordinate rides.
  • Device notification identifier — a push-notification token so we can send you ride alerts and reminders. You can turn notifications off any time in Settings.

We do not collect your device's precise GPS location — locations in the app are the named places and landmarks you type in. We do not sell your personal data, and the app contains no advertising.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To match, display, and coordinate shared rides between residents.
  • To send realtime notifications about seat requests, approvals, cancellations, and reminders.
  • To enable in-app chat between riders.
  • To keep the service secure and working correctly.

How your information is stored and shared

  • App data (account, profile, rides, messages) is stored using Supabase, our backend and database provider, which processes this data on our behalf.
  • Push notifications are delivered through the platform notification service (Google/Firebase on Android) using your notification token.
  • Other residents can see the profile and ride details you choose to share (for example, your name and a ride you offer) so that rides can be coordinated.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time — see Delete your account or email prashobhpaulnambadan@gmail.com. We action deletion requests within 30 days, except where we must retain data to comply with legal obligations.

Children

RVonWheelz is intended for adult residents commuting to work and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
